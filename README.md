## The Features That Actually Matter

Not every feature in a tool's list is worth reading. Here are the ones at Woodpecker that genuinely change how cold email performs:

**Free Email Verification** — Powered by Bouncer, Woodpecker checks every address before you send. Invalid emails, catch-all addresses, misspelled domains — they get filtered out automatically. This alone keeps your bounce rate low and your sender reputation intact.

**Free Email Warm-Up** — Before you start blasting cold emails, your domain needs to look trustworthy to email providers. Woodpecker's warm-up gradually builds that reputation in the background, without you having to think about it.

**Inbox Rotation** — Instead of sending everything from one address (which screams "automated" to spam filters), Woodpecker spreads your campaigns across multiple inboxes. You can add as many email accounts as you want at no extra charge.

**Adaptive Sending** — The platform watches your sending patterns in real time and adjusts the pace so you never hit your email provider's limits. No manual throttling, no blocked accounts.

**Condition-Based Campaigns** — This is where it gets smart. You set conditions, and the campaign adapts. If someone opens your email but doesn't reply, they get one follow-up. If they click a link, they get a different one. The sequences branch and adjust based on actual behavior.

**A/B Testing** — Test up to 5 versions of any email in a sequence. Woodpecker tells you which one performs best, so you're not guessing.

**AI Email Writing Assistant** — Stuck on what to write? The built-in AI drafts cold email copy you can edit and send. It's a starting point, not a replacement for your voice, but it cuts the time to first draft significantly.

**LinkedIn Outreach Automation** — This is one of Woodpecker's newest features. You can now add LinkedIn profile visits, connection requests, and direct messages directly into your email sequences. If someone accepts your connection request, the campaign can automatically shift to sending a LinkedIn message instead of another email. The LinkedIn add-on runs at $29/month per account.

**Bottom line:** Woodpecker is a strong choice if cold email deliverability is your top priority and you want something that's relatively easy to pick up and use. It's not the cheapest option on the market, but for the teams where it clicks, it becomes a core part of their outbound stack.
